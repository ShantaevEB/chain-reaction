Privacy Policy for Chain Reaction

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Chain Reaction is an offline single-player puzzle game. This policy explains what the game stores and the choices available to you. The publisher and support contact is Eduard Shantaev, supportebsg@gmail.com.

Information stays in the app

The game does not send personal information or gameplay data to the publisher. It has no account registration, advertising, analytics, tracking SDKs, online rankings, social sharing or multiplayer services. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts or location.

The game stores the following information in its local app storage so you can continue playing:

  • Campaign progress, level ratings, scores, attempt counts and selected level variants.
  • The earned-star award history, cosmetic unlocks and selected appearance, including any applicable legacy appearance grant.
  • Music, sound, haptics, effects and language preferences, and whether the introduction has been completed.
  • Daily challenge scores, aggregate personal records such as best scores, captured-orb totals, fastest completion times and win counts, and completed contract or practice identifiers.
  • A private laboratory field if you save one.

The game does not record a sequence of your taps, save a replay of a successful attempt, or upload your laboratory fields. Daily and weekly challenges are determined on your device from the calendar; the app does not contact a challenge server. Window and fold geometry are used to arrange the interface, rather than to create a tracking profile.

Purchases and third parties

Cosmetics use stars earned by playing the campaign. The app has no in-app purchases or subscription service and does not ask for payment-card information. An App Store download may be subject to the price shown by Apple; this policy does not determine that price.

There are no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs in the app. Artwork and music are bundled with the game; playing does not send prompts, images or audio to an AI service. Apple operates the App Store, system services and any device backups you enable under Apple's own terms and privacy policy. Device backups may include local app data according to your Apple settings; the publisher cannot access those backups through the game. The app does not implement its own cloud sync or publisher-operated backup.

Your choices and retention

Local information remains available until it is changed or removed in the app or through the operating system. In Settings you can change sound, music, haptics, effects and language without creating an account.

Reset Progress clears current campaign results and progression, attempts, selected campaign variants, daily scores, personal records and completed contract or practice marks. It deliberately preserves the earned-star award history, cosmetic purchases and applicable legacy appearance grants, preferences, and any saved laboratory field. This keeps previous cosmetic unlocks and prevents the same campaign stars from being awarded again after a reset.

To remove all local game information, use Delete App in iOS or iPadOS. Offloading the app can retain its data and is different from deleting it. If you later restore a device backup, local game data may be restored by Apple. Manage or delete those backups using Apple's device and account settings. We cannot retrieve, edit or delete a save on your device remotely because the game does not send it to us.

Support and children

The game does not ask for a date of birth or collect children's information. It has no chat, user profiles or user-generated content service. An App Store age rating describes content suitability; it is not an account or age-verification feature.

If you choose to contact the publisher using the support page, the information you voluntarily provide is outside the game's local save. Provide only what is necessary to explain your request. We use the message and your contact details to respond, do not use them for advertising, and retain them only as needed to resolve the request or meet applicable obligations. Do not send private device backups or sensitive personal information. Support emails are handled using Gmail. Google processes email under its privacy policy. This website does not include a contact form.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not add analytics, advertising scripts, tracking pixels, cookies or browser storage. Images and styles are served with the website; no external fonts or embedded media are loaded. Visiting the site still sends technical request data to GitHub. GitHub states that it logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security, even when they are not signed in. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement. The game’s offline operation does not mean that the hosting provider receives no website request data.

Changes and contact

If the game's privacy practices change, the policy and relevant App Store privacy disclosures will be updated. The date above identifies this version of the policy.

Privacy questions and support: contact the publisher through the support page.
