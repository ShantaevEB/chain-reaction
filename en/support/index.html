What to include

  • Your device model and iOS or iPadOS version.
  • The game version, the level or mode, and what happened.
  • Steps to reproduce the issue and a screenshot, if helpful.

Do not send passwords, payment details or private device backups. Please check screenshots for personal information before attaching them.

Common questions

Does the game need an internet connection?

No. Campaign levels and additional modes work offline. Daily challenges are determined on your device from the calendar.

How do I unlock visual themes?

Improve your campaign ratings to earn stars. The shop uses those stars. There are no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

What does Reset Progress remove?

It clears campaign results, progression and personal records. Earned-star history, cosmetic unlocks, settings and your saved laboratory field remain. See the privacy policy for full details.

Can you restore my save?

The game does not send us a copy of your progress. We cannot remotely retrieve or edit a local save. Apple device backups may include app data according to your settings.
